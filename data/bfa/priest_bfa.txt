BFA PRIEST (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Divine Toll: Massive Holy Power / Resource generation.
- Wake of Ashes: Burst damage (Shadow).
- Beacon of Virtue: High throughput, high mana.
- Power Infusion: Essential haste buff.
- Halo: AoE burst.

========================================================================
SPEC: HOLY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Managing Holy Words and Prayer of Mending.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Holy Word: Serenity: Primary target heal.
2. Holy Word: Sanctify: Primary AoE heal.
3. Prayer of Mending: High priority for throughput.
4. Flash Heal: Filler (especially with Surge of Light).
5. Prayer of Healing: Group heal.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Surge of Light: Instant/Free Flash Heal.

COOLDOWNS:
- Divine Hymn: Massive group healing.
- Apotheosis: Enhanced healing throughput.
- Guardian Spirit: Emergency save.

========================================================================
SPEC: DISCIPLINE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Atonement and resource-driven healing.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Power Word: Shield: Apply Atonement.
2. Penance: Primary Atonement tool.
3. Power Word: Radiance: AoE Atonement.
4. Smite / Holy Fire: Filler for Atonement healing.
5. Shadow Word: Pain: Maintain for damage.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Atonement: Essential for any healing to occur.

COOLDOWNS:
- Divine Toll: Resource burst.
- Power Word: Barrier: Damage reduction.
- Rapture: Shield-based burst heal.

========================================================================
SPEC: SHADOW (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Insanity management and Void Eruption windows.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Vampiric Touch: Maintain on all targets.
2. Mind Blast: Primary generator.
3. Devouring Plague: Primary spender.
4. Shadow Word: Death: Execute (< 20% health).
5. Mind Flay / Mind Sear: Filler (ST / AoE).

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Voidform: Priority shifts to Void Bolt on cooldown.

COOLDOWNS:
- Void Eruption: Enter Voidform.
- Mindbender: Burst damage and resource gen.
- Power Infusion: Haste burst.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL PRIEST)
========================================================================
- Utility: Psychic Scream, Mass Dispel, Mind Control.
- Defensive: Fade, Power Word: Shield.
- Shadow PvP: Use Mindbender and Voidform for burst pressure.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Shadow.
- Loop: Vampiric Touch -> Mind Blast -> Devouring Plague.
- Use Void Eruption on cooldown.
