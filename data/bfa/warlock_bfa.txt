BFA WARLOCK (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Affliction: Nightfall, Phantom Singularity, Soul Swap.
- Demonology: Demonspine, Tyrant efficiency.
- Destruction: Chaos Bolt, Havoc, Rain of Fire.
- Global: Healthstone / Soulstone.

========================================================================
SPEC: AFFLICTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Complex DoT rotations and "Seed" management.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Agony: Maintain 100% uptime.
2. Corruption: Maintain on target.
3. Unstable Affliction: Maintain high damage DoT.
4. Drain Soul: Primary filler/finisher.
5. Soul Swap: Efficient DoT transfer.

COOLDOWNS:
- Seed of Corruption: Core AoE tool.
- Summon Darkglare: Massive burst window.
- Soul Rot: High-impact AoE/DoT.

========================================================================
SPEC: DEMONOLOGY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Maximizing the number of active demons and Tyrant bursts.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Hand of Gul'dan: Primary shard spender and demon generator.
2. Call Dreadstalkers: High priority summon.
3. Demonbolt: Filler to maintain shards.
4. Demonflare: Shard expenditure for burst.
5. Vile Taint: AoE setup.

COOLDOWNS:
- Summon Demonic Tyrant: The ultimate burst cooldown.
- Metamorphosis: High-damage transformation.
- Demon Seed: AoE shard generation.

========================================================================
SPEC: DESTRUCTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Heavy-hitting Chaos Bolts and efficient AoE.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Immolate: Maintain on target.
2. Chaos Bolt: Primary shard spender.
3. Conflagrate: Shard generator and cast speed buff.
4. Incinerate: Primary filler.
5. Rain of Fire: Core AoE.

COOLDOWNS:
- Havoc: Essential for multi-target damage.
- Summon Infernal: High AoE burst.
- Metamorphosis: Maximum burst window.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL WARLOCK)
========================================================================
- Utility: Fear, Spell Lock (Interrupt), Healthstone.
- Defensive: Unending Resolve, Soulstone.
- CC: Fear / Soul Rot.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Destruction for high burst/efficiency.
- Loop: Immolate -> Build Shards -> Chaos Bolt.
- Use Voidwalker for tanking.
