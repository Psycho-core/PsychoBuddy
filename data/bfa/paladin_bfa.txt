BFA PALADIN (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Beacon of Virtue: High HPS, high mana cost.
- Beacon of Faith: Stable, low mana cost.
- Divine Toll: Massive HP generation.
- Wake of Ashes: Burst damage and HP generation.
- Final Verdict: High single-target damage spender.

========================================================================
SPEC: HOLY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Balancing Holy Power generation (Holy Shock/Flash of Light) with spending (Light of Dawn/Eternal Flame).
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Holy Shock: Primary generator and spot heal.
2. Light of Dawn: Primary HP spender for raid/group.
3. Eternal Flame: HP spender for single target/focused healing.
4. Flash of Light: Filler generator.
5. Judgment: Maintain buff and generate resources.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Beacon of Faith/Virtue: Maintain on primary tank.

COOLDOWNS:
- Avenging Wrath: Major burst healing window.
- Divine Toll: Instant HP generation.

========================================================================
SPEC: PROTECTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Generating Holy Power to fuel Shield of the Righteous for damage reduction.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Hammer of Wrath: Highest priority generator.
2. Judgment: Secondary generator.
3. Avenger's Shield: Third priority generator.
4. Hammer of the Righteous / Blessed Hammer: Filler generator.
5. Shield of the Righteous: Spend HP @ 3-5 charges to avoid overcapping.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Consecration: Maintain uptime for mitigation and damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Avenging Wrath: Damage and mitigation boost.
- Divine Toll: Burst HP generation.
- Hammer of Light: High impact burst.

========================================================================
SPEC: RETRIBUTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Holy Power management. Maximize generators and avoid overcapping before using high-impact spenders.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Blade of Justice: Priority generator.
2. Judgment: Secondary generator.
3. Hammer of Wrath: Use on cooldown / execute phase.
4. Final Verdict: Primary ST spender (@ 5 HP).
5. Divine Storm: Primary AoE spender (@ 5 HP).

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Art of War: Procs instant Hammer of Wrath.

COOLDOWNS:
- Avenging Wrath: Primary burst window.
- Divine Toll: Instant HP generation.
- Wake of Ashes: Massive burst and HP generation.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL PALADIN)
========================================================================
- Utility: Hand of Freedom, Hand of Protection, Rebuke.
- Defensive: Divine Shield, Lay on Hands.
- Ret PvP: Burst rotation: Wings -> Wake of Ashes -> Divine Toll -> Final Verdict spam.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Retribution.
- Loop: Judgment -> Blade of Justice -> Final Verdict.
- Use Divine Storm for 3+ mobs.
