BFA ROGUE (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Subterfuge: Use stealth abilities for 3s after breaking stealth.
- Deeper Stratagem: Increases CP cap to 6; finishers deal more damage.
- Cheat Death: Prevents death once every 6 min.
- Shadow Dance: (Subtlety) Access stealth abilities in combat.
- Roll the Bones: (Outlaw) Random combat enhancements.
- Symbols of Death: (Subtlety) Massive damage boost to finishers.

========================================================================
SPEC: ASSASSINATION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Maintaining high-damage bleeds and poisons.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Garrote: Maintain bleed and debuff.
2. Rupture: Maintain bleed.
3. Mutilate: Primary CP generator.
4. Envenom: Primary finisher; maintains Slice and Dice.
5. Kingsbane: Massive burst damage window.

COOLDOWNS:
- Deathmark: Huge damage multiplier for a window.
- Vendetta: Single target damage boost.

========================================================================
SPEC: OUTLAW (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Roll the Bones management and high energy expenditure.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Roll the Bones: Maintain Stage 2+ buffs.
2. Between the Eyes: High priority finisher.
3. Dispatch: Primary finisher.
4. Pistol Shot: Use with Opportunity procs.
5. Sinister Strike / Saber Slash: Primary CP generators.

COOLDOWNS:
- Adrenaline Rush: Energy and haste boost.
- Killing Spree: Rapid attack burst.

========================================================================
SPEC: SUBTLETY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Burst windows utilizing Shadow Dance and Symbols of Death.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Shadowstrike: Primary builder (during Dance).
2. Secret Technique: Highest priority finisher (during Dance).
3. Eviscerate / Rupture: Finishers.
4. Backstab: Builder outside of Shadow Dance.
5. Shuriken Storm: AoE builder.

COOLDOWNS:
- Shadow Dance: Core burst window.
- Symbols of Death: Enhances finishers.
- Shadow Blades: Increases CP generation.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL ROGUE)
========================================================================
- Utility: Kidney Shot, Blind, Cheap Shot.
- Defensive: Cloak of Shadows, Evasion.
- Stealth: Vanish for repositioning.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Outlaw for efficiency and AoE.
- Loop: Stealth -> Opener -> CP Build -> Finisher.
- Use Vanish to reset openers.
