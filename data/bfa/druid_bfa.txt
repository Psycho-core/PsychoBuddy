BFA DRUID (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Efflorescence: Ground-based AoE healing.
- Convoke the Spirits: Massive burst of spells (All specs).
- Heart of the Wild: Stat boost and form-specific empowerment.
- Bloodtalons: Buffs melee damage after Regrowth.
- Soul of the Forest: Resource/Damage bonus on finishers.

========================================================================
SPEC: BALANCE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Eclipse Loop and Astral Power spending.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Sunfire / Moonfire: Maintain on targets.
2. Eclipse Entry: 2x Wrath (Lunar) / 2x Starfire (Solar).
3. Spend Astral Power: Starfall (AoE) / Starsurge (ST).
4. Empowered Spells: Spam the current Eclipse-buffed spell.
5. Filler: Wrath / Starfire.

COOLDOWNS:
- Celestial Alignment: Dual Eclipse.
- Convoke the Spirits: Massive Astral Power / Spell dump.

========================================================================
SPEC: FERAL (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Energy/Combo Point management and snapshotting bleeds.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Savage Roar: Maintain at all times.
2. Rake: Maintain bleed.
3. Rip: Use at 5 Combo Points.
4. Shred: Primary CP generator.
5. Ferocious Bite: Use at 5 CP if Rip is active.
6. Tiger's Fury: Use to avoid energy capping / boost damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Incarnation: King of the Jungle: Burst window.
- Convoke the Spirits: High-speed ability chain.

========================================================================
SPEC: GUARDIAN (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Rage generation for Ironfur and Maul.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Thrash: Maintain stacks.
2. Mangle: Primary generator.
3. Moonfire: Maintain for rage gen / damage.
4. Ironfur: Spend rage for Armor (High Priority).
5. Maul: Rage dump for damage.
6. Swipe: Filler AoE.

COOLDOWNS:
- Incarnation: Guardian of Ursoc: Burst mitigation/damage.
- Survival Instincts: Major damage reduction.
- Convoke the Spirits: Burst healing/damage.

========================================================================
SPEC: RESTORATION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: HoT management and ground-based healing.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Efflorescence: Maintain under the group.
2. Lifebloom: Maintain on tank.
3. Wild Growth: Primary AoE heal.
4. Rejuvenation: Blanket the group.
5. Swiftmend: Spot heal.
6. Regrowth: High-priority single target.

COOLDOWNS:
- Tranquility: Massive AoE heal.
- Convoke the Spirits: Rapid fire healing spells.
- Flourish: Extend HoT durations.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL DRUID)
========================================================================
- Utility: Typhoon, Mighty Bash, Entangling Roots.
- Mobility: Dash, Stampeding Roar.
- Survival: Barkskin, Survival Instincts.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Feral (Melee) or Balance (Ranged).
- Feral Loop: Rake -> Shred -> Rip.
- Balance Loop: Sunfire -> Moonfire -> Wrath.
