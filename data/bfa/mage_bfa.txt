BFA MAGE (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Sunfury Abilities: Check if Sunfury hero spec is active (Arcane Phoenix).
- Frostfire Abilities: Check if Frostfire hero spec is active (Frostfire Bolt).
- Shimmer / Alter Time: Check active talent tree.
- Comet Storm / Glacial Spike: Check Frost talent tree.

========================================================================
SPEC: ARCANE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Mana management and Arcane Charge stacks.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Arcane Power / Rune of Power: Use for burst windows.
2. Arcane Blast: Build to 4 charges.
3. Arcane Missiles: Use on procs (Clearcasting).
4. Arcane Barrage: Dump charges and deal AoE.
5. Arcane Explosion: Use for multi-target charge building.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Clearcasting: Procs Arcane Missiles.

COOLDOWNS:
- Arcane Power: Major burst.
- Presence of Mind: Instant cast.

========================================================================
SPEC: FIRE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Hot Streak and Combustion.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Combustion: Primary burst.
2. Pyroblast: Use during Hot Streak.
3. Fire Blast: Generate Hot Streak.
4. Frostfire Bolt: Primary filler.
5. Scorch: Use when moving.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Hot Streak: Triggered by crits.

COOLDOWNS:
- Combustion: Guaranteed crits.

========================================================================
SPEC: FROST (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Splinter generation and Glacial Spike shatters.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Frozen Orb: Use on cooldown.
2. Ray of Frost / Comet Storm: High priority burst.
3. Flurry: Use with Brain Freeze proc.
4. Ice Lance: Use with Fingers of Frost.
5. Glacial Spike: Use when charges are full and target is chilled.
6. Frostbolt: Filler.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Fingers of Frost: Procs Ice Lance.
- Brain Freeze: Procs Flurry.

COOLDOWNS:
- Icy Veins: Primary burst.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL MAGE)
========================================================================
- Opener: Root/Slow $\rightarrow$ Burst.
- Utility: Counterspell, Polymorph, Blink/Shimmer.
- Arcane PvP: Focus on burst and distance.
- Fire PvP: High burst and pressure.
- Frost PvP: Maximum control and kiting.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Level 1-10: Basic spells.
- Level 10-120:
    - Frost: Best for solo questing (AoE and survivability).
    - Fire/Arcane: Higher single-target burst but more fragile.
- General Loop: Frostbolt $\rightarrow$ Ice Lance $\rightarrow$ Flurry.
- Use Mirror Image and Ice Block to survive high-pulls.
