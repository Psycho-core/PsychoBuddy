BFA DEMON HUNTER (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Convoke the Spirits: Massive burst of abilities (All specs).
- Essence Break: Core to the Havoc burst window.
- Inertia: Enhances abilities after movement (Replacement for Momentum).
- Soul Carver: (Vengeance) High impact resource spender.
- Fiery Brand: (Vengeance) Targeted damage reduction.

========================================================================
SPEC: HAVOC (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Fury management and "Inertia" burst windows.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Eye Beam: Primary generator and triggers burst windows.
2. Essence Break: Pair with Eye Beam for massive damage.
3. Blade Dance / Death Sweep: High priority AoE spender.
4. Chaos Strike / Annihilation: Primary ST spender.
5. Immolation Aura: Maintain for Fury and AoE damage.
6. Fel Rush: Use for mobility and Fury generation.

COOLDOWNS:
- Metamorphosis: Major burst transformation.
- Chaos Blades: Massive damage increase.
- Convoke the Spirits: Instant chain of high-impact abilities.

========================================================================
SPEC: VENGEANCE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Builder/Spender loop using Soul Fragments and Fury.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Spirit Bomb: Top priority spender for damage and healing.
2. Immolation Aura: Maintain on cooldown for Fury and Souls.
3. Fracture: Primary generator of Fury and Soul Fragments.
4. Soul Cleave: Spend Fury for healing/damage.
5. Demon Spikes: Maintain 100% uptime for mitigation.
6. Infernal Strike: Use for mobility and AoE damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Metamorphosis: Major defensive/offensive boost.
- Fiery Brand: Targeted damage reduction.
- Convoke the Spirits: Burst of abilities for healing/damage.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL DEMON HUNTER)
========================================================================
- Utility: Sigil of Silence, Imprisonment.
- Defensive: Blur, Darkness.
- Havoc PvP: Burst windows and high mobility.
- Vengeance PvP: Tanking and sustain.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Havoc for speed and AoE.
- Loop: Eye Beam -> Blade Dance -> Chaos Strike.
- Use Metamorphosis on cooldown.
