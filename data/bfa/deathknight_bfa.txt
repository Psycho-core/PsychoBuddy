BFA DEATH KNIGHT (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Blood: Death Strike, Bone Shield, Blood Boil.
- Frost: Obliterate, Frost Strike, Howling Blast.
- Unholy: Death Coil, Scourge Strike, Festering Strike.
- Global: Death Grip, Anti-Magic Shell.

========================================================================
SPEC: BLOOD (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: High-impact self-healing and Bone Shield management.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Death Strike: Top priority for survival.
2. Bone Shield: Mandatory uptime.
3. Blood Boil: Core AoE and rune gen.
4. Heart Strike: Primary rune spender.
5. Death and Decay: AoE setup.

COOLDOWNS:
- Vampiric Blood: Burst health/healing.
- Icebound Fortitude: Major damage reduction.
- Bone Shield (Enhanced): Talents for shield efficiency.

========================================================================
SPEC: FROST (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Pillar of Frost windows and Runic Power efficiency.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Obliterate: Primary rune spender.
2. Frost Strike: Primary Runic Power spender.
3. Howling Blast: AoE/Rune gen.
4. Killing Machine (Proc): Priority Obliterate.
5. Frost Fever: Disease maintenance.

COOLDOWNS:
- Pillar of Frost: The ultimate damage window.
- Empower Rune Weapon: Rune regeneration.
- Breath of Sindragosa: Massive Runic Power burn.

========================================================================
SPEC: UNHOLY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Festering Wound management and Army bursts.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Festering Strike: Build wounds.
2. Scourge Strike: Consume wounds.
3. Death Coil: Runic Power spender.
4. Unholy Frenzy: Maintain disease.
5. Army of the Dead: Core summon burst.

COOLDOWNS:
- Army of the Dead: Massive AoE/Burst.
- Summon Gargoyle: Single target burst.
- Empower Rune Weapon: Rune regeneration.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL DEATH KNIGHT)
========================================================================
- Utility: Death Grip, Mind Freeze, Asphyxiate.
- Defensive: Anti-Magic Shell, Icebound Fortitude.
- Mobility: Death Leap.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Blood for soloing efficiency, Frost for speed.
- Loop: Disease -> Build Runes -> Spend on Obliterate/Scourge Strike.
- Use Death Grip to group enemies.
