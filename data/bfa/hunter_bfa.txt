BFA HUNTER (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Convoke the Spirits: Massive burst of abilities (All specs).
- Double Tap: (MM) Critical for burst cycles.
- Trick Shots: (MM) Essential for AoE cleave.
- Mongoose Bite: (SV) Core damage ability.
- Wildfire Bomb: (SV) AoE fire damage and debuff.

========================================================================
SPEC: BEAST MASTERY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Focus management and maintaining Pet Frenzy.
PRIORITY LIST (Single Target):
1. Bestial Wrath: Use on cooldown.
2. Kill Command: Top priority (do not cap charges).
3. Barbed Shot: Maintain Frenzy stacks on pet.
4. Cobra Shot: Filler to avoid focus capping.
5. Kill Shot: Execute phase (< 20% HP).

PRIORITY LIST (AoE):
1. Barbed Shot: Keep Frenzy active.
2. Multi-Shot: Trigger Beast Cleave.
3. Kill Command: Use on cooldown.
4. Bestial Wrath: Use on cooldown.

COOLDOWNS:
- Bestial Wrath: Primary damage boost.
- Aspect of the Wild: Focus and sustain.
- Convoke the Spirits: Massive burst dump.

========================================================================
SPEC: MARKSMANSHIP (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Focus generation and "Precise Shots" spending.
PRIORITY LIST (Single Target):
1. Double Tap: Use on cooldown to enable burst.
2. Rapid Fire: Use on cooldown to generate focus.
3. Aimed Shot: Primary damage (use during Double Tap/Trueshot).
4. Arcane Shot: Use to spend Precise Shots procs.
5. Steady Shot: Filler for focus generation.

PRIORITY LIST (AoE):
1. Multi-Shot: Essential to trigger "Trick Shots".
2. Rapid Fire: Maintain Bulletstorm.
3. Aimed Shot: Use while Trick Shots is active.
4. Volley: Use for consistent AoE damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Trueshot: Massive burst window.
- Double Tap: Grants extra Aimed Shots.
- Convoke the Spirits: Burst of shots.

========================================================================
SPEC: SURVIVAL (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Melee combat, "Mongoose Fury" stacks, and bomb management.
PRIORITY LIST (Single Target):
1. Kill Command: Priority generator.
2. Mongoose Bite: Use when stacks are high.
3. Wildfire Bomb: Use on cooldown.
4. Raptor Strike: Primary spender/filler.
5. Serpent Sting: Maintain DoT.

PRIORITY LIST (AoE):
1. Wildfire Bomb: AoE fire damage.
2. Carve: Maintain AoE bleed.
3. Kill Command: Generator.
4. Butcher/Explosive Shot: Burst AoE.

COOLDOWNS:
- Aspect of the Eagle: Damage and range boost.
- Coordinated Assault: Burst damage window.
- Convoke the Spirits: Rapid chain of melee/ranged attacks.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL HUNTER)
========================================================================
- Utility: Freezing Trap, Intimidation, Misdirection.
- Defensive: Aspect of the Turtle, Disengage.
- BM PvP: High pet pressure.
- MM PvP: Long range burst.
- SV PvP: Melee pressure and traps.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Beast Mastery for simplicity and pet tanking.
- Loop: Kill Command -> Barbed Shot -> Cobra Shot.
- Use Bestial Wrath on cooldown.
