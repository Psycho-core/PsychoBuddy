BFA MONK (8.3.7) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Windwalker: Fists of Fury, Rising Sun Kick, Blackout Kick.
- Brewmaster: Keg Smash, Purifying Brew, Celestial Brew.
- Mistweaver: Renewing Mist, Vivify, Essence Font.
- Global: Zen Meditation.

========================================================================
SPEC: WINDWALKER (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Combo-Strike rule and energy management.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Rising Sun Kick: Top priority damage.
2. Fists of Fury: Core burst/AoE.
3. Blackout Kick: Primary builder.
4. Tiger Palm: Builder (ensure no repeats).
5. Keg Smash: AoE builder.

COOLDOWNS:
- Storm, Earth, and Fire: Burst window.
- Xuen, the White Tiger: Sustained damage.
- Energize: Energy refill.

========================================================================
SPEC: BREWMASTER (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Stagger management and purity.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Keg Smash: Mandatory uptime.
2. Blackout Kick: Primary builder/damage.
3. Breath of Fire: AoE damage.
4. Celestial Brew: Stagger mitigation.
5. Purifying Brew: Stagger clearing.

COOLDOWNS:
- Fortifying Brew: Major damage reduction.
- Zen Meditation: Magic cleanse/reduction.
- Diffuse Magic: Magic mitigation.

========================================================================
SPEC: MISTWEAVER (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Fistweaving (damage-to-heal) and targeted HoTs.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Renewing Mist: Primary HoT.
2. Vivify: Burst heal.
3. Essence Font: Use on cooldown.
4. Enveloping Mist: Strong single target.
5. Soothing Mist: Filler heal.

COOLDOWNS:
- Revival: Group-wide burst heal.
- Life Cocoon: High-priority shield.
- Zen Sphere: AoE utility.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL MONK)
========================================================================
- Utility: Leg Sweep, Paralysis, Spear Hand Strike.
- Defensive: Touch of Karma, Fortifying Brew.
- Mobility: Roll, Flying Serpent Kick.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-120)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Windwalker for efficiency.
- Loop: Tiger Palm -> Blackout Kick -> Rising Sun Kick.
- Use mobility skills to skip unimportant mobs.
