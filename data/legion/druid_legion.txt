LEGION DRUID (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Flourish: Extends duration of all HoTs.
- Incarnation: (All specs) Massive burst/utility window.
- Astral Mastery: Reduces cast time of empowered spells.
- Bloodtalons: Melee abilities deal more damage after Regrowth/Roots.
- Soul of the Forest: Finishing abilities deal more damage / generate energy.

========================================================================
SPEC: BALANCE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Eclipse Cycle (Lunar/Solar) and Astral Power management.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Sunfire / Moonfire: Maintain on all targets.
2. Enter Eclipse: Cast 2 Wraths (for Lunar) or 2 Starfires (for Solar).
3. Spend Astral Power: Starfall (AoE) or Starsurge (ST).
4. Empowered Spell: Spam the spell buffed by the current Eclipse.
5. Starfire / Wrath: Filler to build Astral Power.

COOLDOWNS:
- Celestial Alignment: Enters both eclipses simultaneously.
- Incarnation: Chosen of Elune: Extreme burst window.

========================================================================
SPEC: FERAL (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Combo Points and Bleed maintenance.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Savage Roar: Maintain at all times (increases damage/energy regen).
2. Rake: Maintain bleed on target.
3. Rip: Use at 5 Combo Points.
4. Shred: Primary CP generator.
5. Ferocious Bite: Use at 5 CP if Rip is active.
6. Tiger's Fury: Use when energy is low (< 60).

COOLDOWNS:
- Berserk / Incarnation: King of the Jungle: Massive damage boost.

========================================================================
SPEC: GUARDIAN (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Active Mitigation (Ironfur) and AoE Threat.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Thrash: Maintain stacks and AoE threat.
2. Mangle: Primary rage generator and ST damage.
3. Swipe: Filler AoE threat.
4. Ironfur: Spend rage to increase Armor (Maintain 100% uptime).
5. Maul: High rage dump (if not needing Ironfur).

COOLDOWNS:
- Barkskin: 20% damage reduction.
- Survival Instincts: Major damage reduction.
- Rage of the Sleeper: Damage reflection and mitigation.

========================================================================
SPEC: RESTORATION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Healing over Time (HoT) blanket.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Lifebloom: Maintain on tank or priority target.
2. Rejuvenation: Blanket the group.
3. Wild Growth: Priority AoE heal.
4. Swiftmend: High priority spot heal.
5. Regrowth: Use on high-damage targets or with procs.

COOLDOWNS:
- Tranquility: Massive group heal.
- Flourish: Extend all active HoTs.
- Incarnation: Tree of Life: Enhanced throughput.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL DRUID)
========================================================================
- Utility: Typhoon (Knockback), Mighty Bash (Stun), Entangling Roots (Root).
- Mobility: Dash, Stampeding Roar.
- Survival: Barkskin, Survival Instincts.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Feral for speed/efficiency, Balance for ranged.
- Feral Loop: Rake -> Shred -> Rip.
- Balance Loop: Sunfire -> Moonfire -> Wrath.
