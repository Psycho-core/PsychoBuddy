LEGION HUNTER (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- A Murder of Crows: High priority burst damage.
- Binding Shot: AoE root, useful for add management.
- Killer Cobra: Reducess cooldown of other abilities.
- Double Tap: (MM) Two charges of Aimed Shot.
- Trueshot: (MM) Massive burst window.
- Mongoose Bite: (SV) High damage based on stacks.

========================================================================
SPEC: BEAST MASTERY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Maintaining "Frenzy" on the pet and utilizing Bestial Wrath.
PRIORITY LIST (Single Target):
1. Bestial Wrath: Use on cooldown.
2. Kill Command: High priority, especially with Nature's Ally.
3. Barbed Shot: Maintain Frenzy stacks (do not overcap at 3).
4. Cobra Shot: Filler to avoid focus capping.
5. Dire Beast: Use on cooldown.

PRIORITY LIST (AoE):
1. Barbed Shot: Keep Frenzy active.
2. Wild Thrash: Use on cooldown.
3. Bestial Wrath: Use on cooldown.
4. Kill Command: Use on cooldown.
5. Multi-Shot: Filler.

COOLDOWNS:
- Bestial Wrath: Primary damage boost.
- Aspect of the Wild: Focus regeneration and healing.

========================================================================
SPEC: MARKSMANSHIP (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Focus management and utilizing "Precise Shots" procs.
PRIORITY LIST (Single Target):
1. Trueshot: Use as a major burst window.
2. Rapid Fire: Use on cooldown to generate focus and trigger resets.
3. Aimed Shot: Primary damage dealer (avoid capping charges).
4. Arcane Shot: Use immediately after Aimed Shot to spend Precise Shots.
5. Steady Shot: Filler to generate focus.

PRIORITY LIST (AoE):
1. Multi-Shot: Use to trigger "Trick Shots" (essential for AoE).
2. Rapid Fire: Use to maintain Bulletstorm.
3. Aimed Shot: Use while Trick Shots is active.
4. Volley: Use on cooldown for AoE damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Trueshot: Massive damage increase.
- Double Tap: Grants extra Aimed Shot charges.

========================================================================
SPEC: SURVIVAL (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: "Mongoose Fury" stack management and melee combat.
PRIORITY LIST (Single Target):
1. Mongoose Bite: Use when stacks are high (2+).
2. Flanking Strike: Use to generate Mongoose stacks.
3. Spitting Cobra: Use on cooldown for focus/damage.
4. Lacerate: Maintain bleed on target.
5. Raptor Strike: Filler.

PRIORITY LIST (AoE):
1. Carnage: Use to initiate.
2. Explosive Trap: Use on cooldown.
3. Carve: Maintain AoE bleed.
4. Butchery: Use for high burst AoE.

COOLDOWNS:
- Aspect of the Eagle: Increases damage and range.
- Fury of the Eagle: Burst damage.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL HUNTER)
========================================================================
- Utility: Freezing Trap, Scatter Shot, Intimidation (Pet).
- Defensive: Aspect of the Turtle (Immunity), Disengage.
- BM PvP: Focus on pet pressure and sustain.
- MM PvP: High burst, focus on Trueshot windows.
- SV PvP: High mobility, melee burst and traps.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Beast Mastery for ease of use and pet tanking.
- Loop: Kill Command -> Barbed Shot -> Cobra Shot.
- Use Bestial Wrath on cooldown.
