LEGION DEMON HUNTER (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Essence Break: Huge damage boost to following abilities.
- Momentum: Enhances abilities after Vengeful Retreat.
- Demon Blades: Automates Fury generation.
- Chaos Blades: Massive burst window.
- Spirit Bomb: (Vengeance) High damage and healing.

========================================================================
SPEC: HAVOC (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Fury generation and spending during Metamorphosis windows.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Eye Beam: Primary Fury generator and triggers Chaos Brand.
2. Essence Break: Pair with Eye Beam for massive damage.
3. Blade Dance / Death Sweep: Primary AoE spender.
4. Chaos Strike / Annihilation: Primary ST spender.
5. Immolation Aura: Regular activation for Fury and damage.
6. Fel Rush: Use with Unbound Chaos proc for mobility and damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Metamorphosis: Major burst transformation.
- Chaos Blades: Short-term massive damage increase.

========================================================================
SPEC: VENGEANCE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Resource generation (Fury/Souls) for mitigation and healing.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Metamorphosis: Burst defensive/offensive window.
2. Spirit Bomb: Primary resource spender for damage and healing.
3. Immolation Aura: Maintain for AoE damage and Fury.
4. Fracture: Primary generator of Fury and Soul Fragments.
5. Soul Cleave: Spend Fury for healing and damage.
6. Demon Spikes: Maintain for physical damage reduction.

COOLDOWNS:
- Fiery Brand: Targeted damage reduction.
- Soul Barrier: Absorb shield for survival.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL DEMON HUNTER)
========================================================================
- Utility: Sigil of Silence (AoE Silence), Imprisonment (CC).
- Defensive: Blur (Damage reduction), Darkness (High avoidance).
- Havoc PvP: Burst windows with Eye Beam and Metamorphosis.
- Vengeance PvP: Heavy mitigation and high sustain.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Havoc for fast clearing and mobility.
- Loop: Eye Beam -> Blade Dance -> Chaos Strike.
- Use Metamorphosis on cooldown.
