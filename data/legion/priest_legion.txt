LEGION PRIEST (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Angelic Feather: Mobility boost.
- Divine Star / Halo: AoE heal/damage.
- Surrender to Madness: High-risk, high-reward Shadow burst.
- Chastening: Enhances Penance.
- Power Infusion: Haste boost (Self/Ally).

========================================================================
SPEC: HOLY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Serendipity (casting spells reduces the cooldown of Holy Words).
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Holy Word: Serenity: High priority single target heal.
2. Holy Word: Sanctify: High priority AoE heal.
3. Prayer of Mending: Essential for throughput and Serendipity.
4. Flash Heal: Primary filler / Surge of Light spender.
5. Prayer of Healing: Group heal.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Surge of Light: Makes next Flash Heal instant and free.

COOLDOWNS:
- Apotheosis: Massive increase to Serendipity and healing.
- Divine Hymn: Group heal and cooldown reduction.
- Guardian Spirit: Life-saving cooldown.

========================================================================
SPEC: DISCIPLINE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Atonement (damage dealt heals allies).
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Power Word: Shield: Apply Atonement to target.
2. Penance: Primary Atonement heal and damage.
3. Power Word: Radiance: AoE Atonement application.
4. Smite / Holy Fire: Fill and maintain Atonement healing.
5. Shadow Word: Pain: Maintain for damage and Atonement.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Atonement: Must be active on allies to heal via damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Power Infusion: Haste increase.
- Rapture: Burst healing based on shields.
- Power Word: Barrier: Damage mitigation.

========================================================================
SPEC: SHADOW (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Insanity generation and spending in Void Form.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Vampiric Touch: Maintain on all targets.
2. Shadow Word: Pain: Maintain on all targets.
3. Mind Blast: High priority generator.
4. Devouring Plague: Primary Insanity spender.
5. Mind Flay / Mind Sear: Filler (ST / AoE).

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Void Form: Entered at 100 Insanity. Priority shifts to Void Bolt.
- Surrender to Madness: Extended Void Form until death.

COOLDOWNS:
- Void Eruption: Enter Void Form.
- Mindbender: Pet damage and Insanity generation.
- Power Infusion: Haste burst.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL PRIEST)
========================================================================
- Utility: Psychic Scream (AoE Fear), Mass Dispel, Mind Control.
- Defensive: Fade, Power Word: Shield, Desperate Prayer.
- Shadow PvP: Focus on kiting and burst with Void Form.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Shadow for fastest soloing.
- Loop: Vampiric Touch -> Mind Blast -> Devouring Plague.
- Use Void Form on cooldown.
