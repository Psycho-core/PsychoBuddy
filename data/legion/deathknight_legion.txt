LEGION DEATH KNIGHT (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Blood: Death Strike (Core), Bone Shield (Maintenance), Blood Boil.
- Frost: Obliterate (Core), Frost Strike, Howling Blast.
- Unholy: Death Coil (Core), Scourge Strike, Festering Strike.
- Global: Death Grip (Utility), Anti-Magic Shell (Defensive).

========================================================================
SPEC: BLOOD (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Active mitigation and self-healing via Death Strike.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Death Strike: Highest priority for survival/healing.
2. Bone Shield: Maintain 100% uptime.
3. Blood Boil: Maintain bleed and generate runes.
4. Heart Strike: Filler/Rune expenditure.
5. Death and Decay: AoE damage and rune generation.

COOLDOWNS:
- Vampiric Blood: Massive health increase and healing boost.
- Bone Shield (Talents): Enhancements to shield duration.
- Icebound Fortitude: Damage reduction burst.

========================================================================
SPEC: FROST (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Managing Runic Power and Rune expenditure for Frost damage.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Obliterate: Primary rune spender (High damage).
2. Frost Strike: Primary runic power spender.
3. Howling Blast: AoE and rune generator.
4. Killing Machine (Proc): Guaranteed crit Obliterate.
5. Frost Fever: Maintain disease on target.

COOLDOWNS:
- Pillar of Frost: Massive strength increase.
- Empower Rune Weapon: Rune regeneration boost.
- Breath of Sindragosa (Talent): Sustained runic power burn.

========================================================================
SPEC: UNHOLY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Managing diseases and the Ghoul army.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Festering Strike: Apply festering wounds.
2. Scourge Strike: Consume wounds for high damage.
3. Death Coil: Runic power spender/healing for pets.
4. Unholy Frenzy: Maintain disease.
5. Army of the Dead: Massive burst summon.

COOLDOWNS:
- Army of the Dead: Core burst cooldown.
- Summon Gargoyle: Single target burst.
- Empower Rune Weapon: Rune regeneration.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL DEATH KNIGHT)
========================================================================
- Utility: Death Grip (Pull), Mind Freeze (Interrupt), Asphyxiate (Stun).
- Defensive: Anti-Magic Shell (Magic immunity), Icebound Fortitude.
- Mobility: Death Leap (If available).

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Blood for extreme tankiness, Frost for fast single target.
- Loop: Disease/Bleed -> Rune Build -> High Damage Spender.
- Use Death Grip to gather mobs for AoE.
