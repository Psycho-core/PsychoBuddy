LEGION SHAMAN (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Elemental: Primordial Wave (if available), Stormkeeper (buffs Lightning Bolt).
- Enhancement: Stormstrike (Priority), Lava Lash (Priority), Crash Lightning.
- Restoration: Riptide (Core), Healing Rain (AoE), Chain Heal.
- Global: Bloodlust/Heroism (Crucial raid cooldown).

========================================================================
SPEC: ELEMENTAL (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Managing Lava Burst procs and maintaining Flame Shock.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Lava Burst: Highest priority on proc or cooldown.
2. Flame Shock: Maintain on target.
3. Lightning Bolt: Primary filler.
4. Earth Shock: Use to dump excess capacitance/procs.
5. Chain Lightning: Primary AoE builder.

COOLDOWNS:
- Fire Elemental: Massive damage increase and additional Lava Bursts.
- Bloodlust/Heroism: Haste boost for group.
- Ascendance: Temporary transformation for extreme burst.

========================================================================
SPEC: ENHANCEMENT (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: High-attack speed and priority-based ability usage.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Stormstrike: Primary damage and priority.
2. Lava Lash: High priority, maintain bleed/burn.
3. Crash Lightning: AoE and priority builder.
4. Windfury Totem: Maintain 100% uptime.
5. Lightning Bolt: Use when Stormstrike/Lava Lash are on CD.

COOLDOWNS:
- Feral Spirit: Sustained damage and attack speed.
- Ascendance: Massive damage window.
- Bloodlust/Heroism: Haste boost.

========================================================================
SPEC: RESTORATION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Balancing HoTs (Heals over Time) and burst healing.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Riptide: Maintain on priority targets (HoT).
2. Healing Wave: Primary single-target heal.
3. Chain Heal: Primary group/AoE heal.
4. Healing Rain: Maintain on ground for AoE healing.
5. Healing Stream Totem: Constant low-level group healing.

COOLDOWNS:
- Healing Tide Totem: Massive group burst heal.
- Spirit Link Totem: Damage redistribution and survival.
- Healing Rain (Talent variants): Adaptive AoE healing.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL SHAMAN)
========================================================================
- Utility: Wind Shear (Interrupt), Grounding Totem (Spell absorb), Tremor Totem (Fear/Sleep break).
- Defensive: Astral Shift (Damage reduction), Healing Tide Totem.
- CC: Hex (Polymorph).

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Elemental for ranged AoE, Enhancement for fast single-target.
- Loop: Pull -> AoE/DoT -> Burst finisher.
- Use Ghost Wolf for mobility between mobs.
