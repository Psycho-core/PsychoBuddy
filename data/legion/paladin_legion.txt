LEGION PALADIN (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Grant Faith: High priority heal.
- Blessed Hammer: AoE heal/damage.
- Rule of Law: Range increase for heals and Mastery.
- Divine Purpose: Procs costless HP spenders.
- Judgment of Light: Heals attackers.

========================================================================
SPEC: HOLY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Holy Power generation and spending for powerful burst heals.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Holy Shock: Primary generator and spot heal.
2. Crusade: Major cooldown for haste and damage/healing increase.
3. Light of Dawn: Primary HP spender for group healing.
4. Word of Glory: HP spender for single-target emergency healing.
5. Flash of Light: Filler generator.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Divine Purpose: Makes next HP spender costless.

COOLDOWNS:
- Avenging Wrath: Massive increase to crit and healing.
- Divine Protection: Personal survivability.

========================================================================
SPEC: PROTECTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Active mitigation (Shield of the Righteous) and AoE threat.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Avenger's Shield: Primary threat and mitigation.
2. Judgment: Maintain debuff and generate resources.
3. Blessed Hammer: Use if talented for AoE.
4. Consecration: Maintain uptime for damage reduction.
5. Hammer of the Righteous: Filler threat.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Shield of the Righteous: Use as soon as resources allow to maintain DR.

COOLDOWNS:
- Light of the Protector: Strongest heal (use < 60% HP).
- Guardian of Ancient Kings: Major defensive "wall".
- Vengeful Wrath: Burst damage/threat.

========================================================================
SPEC: RETRIBUTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Holy Power generators and high-impact spenders.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Judgment: Keep on cooldown (highest priority).
2. Justicar's Vengeance: Use on Divine Purpose proc @ 5 HP.
3. Templar's Verdict: Use @ 5 HP (or 4 if Judgment is up).
4. Crusader Strike / Zeal: Use @ 2 charges (Generator).
5. Blade of Justice: Use @ 3 HP or less (Generator).
6. Consecration: Maintain if talented.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Divine Purpose: Procs costless spender.

COOLDOWNS:
- Avenging Wrath: Primary burst window.
- Execution Sentence: High damage cooldown.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL PALADIN)
========================================================================
- Utility: Hand of Freedom (anti-slow), Hand of Protection (physical immunity), Rebuke (interrupt).
- Defensive: Divine Shield (Bubble), Lay on Hands (Full heal).
- Ret PvP: Burst with Wings -> Templar's Verdict -> Hammer of Wrath.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Retribution for fastest soloing.
- Loop: Judgment -> Blade of Justice -> Templar's Verdict.
- Use Consecration for AoE pulls.
