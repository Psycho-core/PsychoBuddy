LEGION WARLOCK (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Affliction: Nightfall (proc), Phantom Singularity (AoE burst).
- Demonology: Demonspine (buff), Felguard/Imp management.
- Destruction: Chaos Bolt (Core), Havoc (Target splitting).
- Global: Healthstone / Soulstone (Essential utility).

========================================================================
SPEC: AFFLICTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Maintaining a suite of DoTs to maximize damage over time.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Agony: Maintain 100% uptime.
2. Corruption: Maintain on target.
3. Unstable Affliction: Maintain for high burst DoT.
4. Drain Soul: Primary finisher/filler.
5. Soul Swap: Move DoTs to new targets.

COOLDOWNS:
- Seed of Corruption: Primary AoE seed.
- Summon Darkglare: Massive damage window for DoTs.
- Soul Rot: Group-wide DoT and energy gain.

========================================================================
SPEC: DEMONOLOGY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Building a demon army to overwhelm the target.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Hand of Gul'dan: Primary demon generator.
2. Demonbolt: Filler to generate shards.
3. Demonflare: High-damage shard spender.
4. Felguard/Imp attacks: Managed via abilities.
5. Call Dreadstalkers: High priority demon summon.

COOLDOWNS:
- Demon Seed: AoE generation.
- Summon Demonic Tyrant: Massive burst window.
- Metamorphosis: Transformation for extreme damage.

========================================================================
SPEC: DESTRUCTION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Using Immolate to fuel high-damage Chaos Bolts.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Immolate: Maintain on target.
2. Chaos Bolt: Primary shard spender (Hard hitter).
3. Incinerate: Primary filler/shard generator.
4. Conflagrate: Use to generate shards and boost Incinerate.
5. Rain of Fire: Primary AoE tool.

COOLDOWNS:
- Havoc: Allows spells to hit two targets.
- Summon Infernal: Massive AoE burst and tanking.
- Metamorphosis: Extreme burst window.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL WARLOCK)
========================================================================
- Utility: Fear (CC), Spell Lock (Interrupt), Healthstone.
- Defensive: Unending Resolve (Damage reduction), Soulstone (Self-res).
- CC: Fear / Seed of Corruption (slow/damage).

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Destruction for fast burst, Affliction for multi-dotting.
- Loop: DoT/Immolate -> Build Shards -> Spend on Chaos Bolt/Demonflare.
- Use Voidwalker for tanking mobs.
