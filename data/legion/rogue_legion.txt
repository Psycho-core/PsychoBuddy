LEGION ROGUE (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Subterfuge: Use stealth abilities for 3s after exiting stealth.
- Deeper Stratagem: Increases CP cap to 6; finishers deal more damage.
- Cheat Death: Prevents death once every 6 min.
- Shadow Dance: (Subtlety) Access stealth abilities in combat.
- Roll the Bones: (Outlaw) Random combat enhancements.
- Symbols of Death: (Subtlety) Massive damage boost to finishers.

========================================================================
SPEC: ASSASSINATION (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Poison and Bleed management (DoT focus).
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Garrote: Maintain bleed and silence.
2. Rupture: Maintain bleed for energy return and damage.
3. Mutilate: Primary CP generator.
4. Envenom: Primary finisher; refreshes Slice and Dice.
5. Toxic Blade: Use on cooldown to boost poison damage.

COOLDOWNS:
- Cold Blood: Guaranteed crit on next attack.
- Vendetta: Massive damage increase to a single target.

========================================================================
SPEC: OUTLAW (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Roll the Bones buffs and high-tempo combat.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Roll the Bones: Maintain Stage 2+ buffs.
2. Between the Eyes: High priority finisher/burst.
3. Dispatch: Primary finisher (when BtE is on CD).
4. Pistol Shot: Use with Opportunity procs (Generator).
5. Saber Slash: Primary CP generator.

COOLDOWNS:
- Adrenaline Rush: Massive energy and haste boost.
- Killing Spree: Rapid-fire attacks for burst damage.

========================================================================
SPEC: SUBTLETY (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Shadow Dance windows and burst sequences.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Shadowstrike: Primary builder (especially during Dance).
2. Secret Technique: Highest priority finisher (during Dance).
3. Eviscerate / Rupture: Finishers based on target health/need.
4. Backstab: Builder outside of Shadow Dance.
5. Shuriken Storm: AoE builder.

COOLDOWNS:
- Shadow Dance: Core burst mechanism.
- Symbols of Death: Enhances finishers.
- Shadow Blades: Increases CP generation and shadow damage.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL ROGUE)
========================================================================
- Utility: Kidney Shot (Stun), Blind, Cheap Shot, Gouge.
- Defensive: Cloak of Shadows (Magic immunity), Evasion (Physical dodge).
- Stealth: Vanish for repositioning and resetting.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Outlaw for ease and AoE, Subtlety for burst.
- Loop: Stealth -> Opener -> CP Build -> Finisher.
- Use Vanish to reset stealth openers on mobs.
