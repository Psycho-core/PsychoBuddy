LEGION MONK (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Windwalker: Fists of Fury (Core), Tiger Palm (Builder), Blackout Kick.
- Brewmaster: Keg Smash (Core), Purifying Brew, Celestial Brew.
- Mistweaver: Soothing Mist, Renewing Mist, Vivify.
- Global: Zen Meditation (Defensive/Cleanse).

========================================================================
SPEC: WINDWALKER (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: The "Combo-Strike" rule (Avoid using the same ability twice in a row).
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Rising Sun Kick: Highest priority damage.
2. Fists of Fury: Core burst/AoE.
3. Blackout Kick: Primary builder/filler.
4. Tiger Palm: Builder (Use to avoid repeating abilities).
5. Keg Smash: AoE builder.

COOLDOWNS:
- Storm, Earth, and Fire: Massive damage window (clones).
- Xuen, the White Tiger: High sustained damage.
- Energize: Energy restoration.

========================================================================
SPEC: BREWMASTER (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Managing Stagger and purging it via brews.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Keg Smash: Maintain 100% uptime for Stagger.
2. Blackout Kick: Primary damage and Stagger generation.
3. Breath of Fire: High priority AoE/Damage.
4. Celestial Brew: Use when Stagger is high.
5. Purifying Brew: Clear Stagger.

COOLDOWNS:
- Fortifying Brew: Massive damage reduction.
3. Zen Meditation: Cleanse and damage reduction.
- Diffuse Magic: Magic damage mitigation.

========================================================================
SPEC: MISTWEAVER (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Weaving healing into attacks (Fistweaving) or pure casting.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Renewing Mist: Maintain on priority targets.
2. Soothing Mist: Focus target heal.
3. Vivify: Burst single target heal.
4. Enveloping Mist: Strong single target heal.
5. Essence Font: Use on cooldown for huge heal.

COOLDOWNS:
- Revival: Massive group heal and cleanse.
- Life Cocoon: Single target shield/heal.
- Zen Sphere: AoE healing/damage.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL MONK)
========================================================================
- Utility: Leg Sweep (AoE Stun), Paralysis (CC), Spear Hand Strike (Interrupt).
- Defensive: Fortifying Brew, Touch of Karma (Damage reflection).
- Mobility: Roll, Flying Serpent Kick.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Recommendation: Windwalker for high speed and burst.
- Loop: Tiger Palm -> Blackout Kick -> Rising Sun Kick.
- Use Roll and Flying Serpent Kick to traverse zones quickly.
