LEGION MAGE (7.3.5) - DATA SHEET

========================================================================
TALENT-DEPENDENT ABILITIES (CHECK BEFORE CASTING)
========================================================================
- Rune of Power: Requires talent.
- Comet Storm: Requires talent.
- Shimmer: Requires talent.
- Mirror Image: Requires talent.
- Alter Time: Requires talent.
- Supernova: Requires talent.

========================================================================
SPEC: ARCANE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Arcane Charges (1-4) and Mana Management.
PRIORITY LIST (Burn Phase):
1. Arcane Power / Rune of Power: Activate first.
2. Arcane Blast: Build to 4 charges.
3. Arcane Missiles: Use when 2+ procs are active.
4. Arcane Blast: Maintain pressure.
5. Arcane Barrage: Use to reset charges and dump damage.

PRIORITY LIST (Conserve Phase):
1. Arcane Blast: Build to 2-3 charges.
2. Arcane Missiles: Use on procs.
3. Arcane Barrage: Use to prevent overcapping.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Clearcasting: Procs Arcane Missiles.
- Mark of Aluneth: High damage proc for Arcane Blast.

COOLDOWNS:
- Arcane Power: Massive damage increase.
- Presence of Mind: Instant cast for next spell.

========================================================================
SPEC: FIRE (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Hot Streak (Guaranteed Critical Pyroblast/Fire Blast).
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Combustion: Primary burst window.
2. Pyroblast: Use during Hot Streak.
3. Fire Blast: Use to generate Hot Streak.
4. Fireball / Frostfire Bolt: Filler to build Hot Streak.
5. Scorch: Use when moving.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Hot Streak: Triggered by critical hits.

COOLDOWNS:
- Combustion: Guarantees crits.
- Fire Keel / Trinkets: Use during Combustion.

========================================================================
SPEC: FROST (PVE)
========================================================================
CORE MECHANIC: Fingers of Frost and Winter's Chill.
PRIORITY LIST:
1. Frozen Orb: Use on cooldown.
2. Ray of Frost / Comet Storm: High priority burst.
3. Flurry: Use with Brain Freeze proc.
4. Ice Lance: Use with Fingers of Frost or Winter's Chill.
5. Frostbolt: Filler to generate procs.

PROCS/CONDITIONALS:
- Fingers of Frost: Procs Ice Lance.
- Brain Freeze: Procs Flurry.
- Winter's Chill: Applied by Flurry/Frostbolt.

COOLDOWNS:
- Icy Veins: Increases cast speed and damage.

========================================================================
PVP LOGIC (GENERAL MAGE)
========================================================================
- Opener: Root/Slow (Frost Nova/Slow) -> Burst.
- Utility: Counterspell (Interrupt), Polymorph (CC), Blink/Shimmer (Mobility).
- Arcane PvP: Focus on burst and kiting. Use Arcane Barrage for high damage.
- Fire PvP: High burst, use Combustion to melt targets. Use Pyroblast to finish.
- Frost PvP: Heavy control and kiting. Maintain slows and freezes.

========================================================================
LEVELING (1-110)
========================================================================
- Level 1-10: Basic spells, Frostbolt, Fireball.
- Level 10+: Spec into Frost for faster AoE and survivability.
- General Loop: Frostbolt $\rightarrow$ Ice Lance (on proc) $\rightarrow$ Flurry.
- Use Mirror Image to distract mobs during high-pulls.
