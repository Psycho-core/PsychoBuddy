Racial Abilities & Passives Data Sheet (Legion 7.3.5 & BFA 8.3.7)

========================================================================
OVERVIEW
========================================================================
Racial abilities must be integrated into the "Priority Rotation Engine" as high-priority modifiers. Active racials should be treated as "Cooldowns" and triggered during burst windows.

========================================================================
HORDE RACES
========================================================================

ORC
- Blood Fury (Active): Increases Attack Power/Spell Power. 
  - Priority: Use at start of burst window.
- Hardiness (Passive): Reduces duration of stun effects.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

TROLL
- Berserking (Active): Increases Haste.
  - Priority: Use during burst window (Stack with class cooldowns).
- Regeneration (Passive): Increased health regeneration.
- Change Note: Stability maintained; Haste value scaled with version.

UNDEAD
- Will of the Forsaken (Active): Removes Fear, Sleep, and Charm.
  - Priority: Emergency trigger (PVP/High-end PVE).
- Cannibalize (Active): Heals by eating corpses.
  - Priority: Use during downtime/leveling.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

TAUREN
- War Stomp (Active): AoE stun.
  - Priority: Crowd Control / Interrupt.
- Endurance (Passive): Increased maximum health.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

BLOOD ELF
- Arcane Torrent (Active): Restores resource (Rage/Energy/Mana) and silences nearby enemies.
  - Priority: Resource filler / Interrupt.
- Magic Resistance (Passive): Reduces chance to be critically hit by spells.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

========================================================================
ALLIANCE RACES
========================================================================

HUMAN
- Will to Survive (Active): Removes Stun.
  - Priority: Emergency trigger (PVP).
- Diplomacy (Passive): Increased reputation gains.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

DWARF
- Stoneform (Active): Removes Bleeds, Poisons, Diseases, and increases Armor.
  - Priority: Defensive trigger (PVE/PVP).
- Find Treasure (Passive): Chance to find extra gold/items.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

NIGHT ELF
- Shadowmeld (Active): Enter stealth, drop combat.
  - Priority: Resetting pulls / Survival.
- Quick Feet (Passive): Increased movement speed.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

GNOME
- Escape Artist (Active): Removes Roots/Snares.
  - Priority: Mobility / Survival.
- Expansive Mind (Passive): Increased maximum mana/energy.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

DRAENEI
- Gift of the Naaru (Active): Heals target.
  - Priority: Emergency self-heal / Utility.
- Heroic Presence (Passive): Increased hit chance (legacy, mostly negligible in modern versions).
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

========================================================================
NEUTRAL RACES
========================================================================

PANDAREN
- Epicurean (Passive): Increased effect from food buffs.
- Quaking Palm (Active): Stuns target.
  - Priority: Crowd Control.
- Change Note: Stable across Legion/BFA.

========================================================================
VERSION COMPARISON & EXTENSIVE CHANGES
========================================================================
Comparing 7.3.5 (Legion) vs 8.3.7 (BFA):

- NO EXTENSIVE MECHANIC CHANGES: The core functionality of racials remained consistent between Legion and BFA.
- SCALING: While the mechanics didn't change, the numerical values (Attack Power from Blood Fury, Haste from Berserking) scaled according to the gear and stat inflation of BFA.
- IMPLEMENTATION NOTE: The Bot's "Priority Engine" should use the Ability ID rather than hardcoded values to ensure compatibility across both versions.
